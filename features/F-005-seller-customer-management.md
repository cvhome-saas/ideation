# Feature: Seller Customer Management & Order Navigation
**ID**: F-005

## Description
New feature for the seller admin service to manage customers and their associated orders.
- Provide a interface to query and filter the customer list based on various criteria.
- Support navigation from a specific customer to a list of their specific orders.
- Enables sellers to better track customer history and provide support.

## Affected Modules
- `backend` (Querying, filtering logic, and customer-order relationship APIs)
- `seller-ui` (Customer list view, filters, and navigation to order history)
